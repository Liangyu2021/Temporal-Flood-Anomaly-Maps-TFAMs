The labeled points used for TabPFN classification were not extracted from the reference flood extents used for the final accuracy evaluation. Instead, flood and non-flood samples were manually interpreted and labeled using a high-resolution Planet optical image acquired over Guangxi on 1 October 2025. Pixels showing clear inundation characteristics and clearly non-inundated conditions were selected, while flood boundaries, cloud shadows, and mixed pixels were avoided where possible. The labels were stored as vector points, with value=1 representing non-flood samples and value=2 representing flood samples. Before classification, these labels were recoded as y=0 for non-flood and y=1 for flood.
